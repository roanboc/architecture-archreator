# Business layer — archreator

_[← EA home](../README.md)_

**ArchiMate viewpoint:** Business layer.

Who does what when a project uses archreator, and what the method actually
offers them. The interesting part is that one of the three actors is an AI
holding a role with real decision rights — which is the convention this
method asks every downstream project to use, applied to itself.
