# 1 — A model rebuilt from scratch on a new version of its own method

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Kind:** initiative
**Delivered:** [Rebuild the models on method 0.2](../scope/1_rebuild-the-models-on-method-02.md)

## What the method did not cover

### Where a rebuild's history goes

The rebuild route said the new documents' `Source` cells cite the
preservation ref. Following it faithfully made every document narrate its
own rebuild — status lines, READMEs, provenance columns — and the Requester
struck all of it: the state and its reason belong on the page, the history
in the initiative record.

### The legend and its table said the same thing twice

Every element document opened with a legend diagram *and* a
glyph/shape/prefix table, as prescribed. On real pages the pair read as two
copies a reader reconciles by matching glyphs.

### Whose capabilities an organization's strategy holds

Discovering the organization that builds a product, the capability areas
came out as the product's abilities — what the method does for adopters —
because nothing said the areas must be the subject's own. The canvas had
carried the answer all along: one area per key activity.

### The map passed for a model

The processes stopped at level 1, each stop justified in a table. The
completeness rule existed — levels 1 and 2 complete across the subject —
but the presentation guidance let a map rationalize itself as deliberate
depth.

### Suppliers drifted into the actor catalogue

The model providers and the code host got actor rows because nothing said a
dependency the organization only relies on is not an actor.

### When one file is too much

The small model keeps a whole layer in one document comfortably; nothing
said where a bigger subject splits, or by what line.

## What was done instead, and why

Each moment was corrected on the initiative's review at the Requester's
direction, and the reasoning in every case was the same shape: the fact was
already somewhere in the method — the canvas mapping, the completeness rule,
the subject-not-construction rule — and the page-level guidance had not
carried it to where the writing happened. The fixes moved the rule to the
point of use rather than inventing new doctrine.

## Does it generalize?

All six — yes, and the generalization call was the Requester's, made
explicitly on the review rather than inferred from a single occurrence by
this note.

## What surprised us

- **The method was teaching the drift it forbids.** The strongest review
  theme — no history in descriptive documents — struck wording the method
  itself had prescribed for rebuilds.
- **A pending review is invisible.** The gates were presented as grantable
  by review reply, and the first sixteen comments sat in a review that was
  never submitted — addressable by reading, unanswerable by reply. The
  presentation survived, through one consolidated comment, but the
  mechanism assumed a submitted review.
- **One nested picture beat the prescribed pair.** The composite domain map
  — objects inside their domain boxes — carried both levels at once, better
  than the flat map and table the patterns called for.

## Deliberately not recorded

Nothing was excluded: no client was involved, and both repositories — the
method's and this one — are public.
