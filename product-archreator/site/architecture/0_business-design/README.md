# Business Design Layer

_[← EA home](../README.md)_

**Not used.** This tree is
[Depth 1 — Application](../README.md#modeling-depth): the subject is one
deliverable, and the canvases here describe an organization's customers and
economics — who is served, what they are trying to get done, what hurts, and
how each offering is paid for. This subject has none of its own.

The folder stays rather than being deleted, because an empty folder that says
why is a known gap and a missing one is an unknown gap.

**Where the canvases are.** One tree up, in
[`org-archreator/`](../../../../org-archreator/architecture/0_business-design/README.md),
which is Depth 2 and does have customers and economics. This tree's strategy
layer is therefore derived directly with the Requester rather than traced back
to a canvas block — which is why
[`1_strategy/`](../1_strategy/README.md) carries no `Source` column.

This folder is not an ArchiMate layer in any case. Its documents would be a
Value Proposition Canvas and a Business Model Canvas, and their elements are
canvas blocks rather than ArchiMate elements.
