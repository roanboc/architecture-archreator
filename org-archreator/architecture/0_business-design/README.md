# Business design

_[← Front door](../README.md)_

**Not an ArchiMate layer.** The two Strategyzer canvases everything else is
derived from: who is served, what hurts them, what is offered against it, and
how the whole thing pays for itself. The
[strategy layer](../1_strategy/README.md) is derived from these documents,
block by block, and never invented alongside them.

| # | Document | Holds |
| - | -------- | ----- |
| 1 | [Value proposition canvas](./1_value-proposition-canvas.md) | Segments, jobs, pains, gains — and the products, relievers and creators offered against them |
| 2 | [Business model canvas](./2_business-model-canvas.md) | Channels, relationships, activities, resources, partners, revenue and cost — and the canvas-to-ArchiMate correspondence |

**Direction** covers this layer: the canvases are approved before anything is
derived from them, as the first of the gate's two sittings.
