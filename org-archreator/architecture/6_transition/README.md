# Roadmap

_[← EA home](../README.md) · [Scope documents](../scope/README.md)_

**ArchiMate viewpoint:** Implementation & Migration (Plateau, Gap).

**Not started.** No target state has been approved for the organization
itself. What this tree describes — the stakeholders, the capability base and
the courses of action — is current state, and the direction it is currently
moving in is carried by the products it builds rather than by a plateau of its
own.

The folder stays rather than being deleted, because an empty folder that says
why is a known gap and a missing one is an unknown gap.

**What would change the answer.** A roadmap belongs here the moment the
organization means to be something it is not yet — a second product line, a
second person who can approve, a capability it has decided to acquire. That is
its own initiative through the `plan-the-transition` skill, approved at
**Gate 1** like any other statement of direction, and it approves no work: every
initiative on the sequence still stops at its own Gate 2.

Until then the only recorded direction lives in
[`product-archreator/`](../../../product-archreator/architecture/6_transition/README.md),
which is where the method's own plateaus are.
